Qt / PySide6 and Shiboken6

The installed PySide6-Essentials and Shiboken6 packages declare:
LGPL-3.0-only OR GPL-2.0-only OR GPL-3.0-only.

This application uses the LGPL alternative and dynamically links the libraries.
The application source is delivered separately. No modifications to Qt, PySide6
or Shiboken6 are made. Their shared libraries are in _internal/PySide6 and
_internal/shiboken6, and may be replaced with compatible builds.
